## 🚀 **Apply Right Now (Takes 15 Minutes)**

### **Step 1: Go to Google AdSense**
1. Visit: https://www.google.com/adsense/
2. Click **"Get Started"**
3. Sign in with your Google account

### **Step 2: Add Your Website**
1. Enter your website URL: `https://yourdomain.com`
2. Select your country: **India**
3. Choose payment currency: **INR (Indian Rupees)**
4. Select: **"I want to monetize one website"**

### **Step 3: Connect Your Site**
Choose **HTML tag method** (easiest):
